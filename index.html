Information Collection and Use We do not collect any personal information about you or your pets on our servers. Types of Data Collected * Pet and Owner Data: Names, ages, weights, medical records, and owner contact information (names and phone numbers) are stored strictly on your device to provide the application's core functionality. * Media Files: The Service requires access to your device's camera and gallery only when you choose to add photos to an animal's profile.
